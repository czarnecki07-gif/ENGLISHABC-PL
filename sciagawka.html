Sygnały czasów

  • Present Simple: always, usually, often, never, every day
  • Present Cont.: now, at the moment, today, look!
  • Past Simple: yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago
  • Past Cont.: at 8 p.m., while, when
  • Present Perfect: already, just, yet, ever, never, since, for
  • Future: tomorrow, next week, soon, in two days

Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Present Perfect – gdy ważny skutek teraz, brak daty.

I have lost my keys. (nie mam ich)

Past Simple – gdy podana data.

I lost my keys yesterday.

Strona bierna

Budowa: be + III forma

The room is cleaned every day.

The car was repaired yesterday.

The work will be finished tomorrow.

The report has been sent.

The problem must be solved.

Have sth done: I had my car repaired.

Stopniowanie

Krótkie: tall → taller → the tallest

Długie: beautiful → more beautiful → the most

Nieregularne:

good → better → the best

bad → worse → the worst

far → further → the furthest

Mowa zależna

Cofamy czas o jeden:

"I am tired" → he was tired

"I work" → he worked

"I will come" → he would come

"I can help" → he could help

Przyimki in / on / at

IN – in May, in 2025, in Poland

ON – on Monday, on 5th May

Some / Any / Much / Many

some – twierdzenia (some water)

much – niepoliczalne (much money)
